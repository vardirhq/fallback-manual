## When to use

Use this when someone gets ill from heat. Heat exhaustion causes tiredness, dizziness, headache, nausea or vomiting, heavy sweating, pale and clammy skin, cramps, strong thirst and a high temperature.

Heatstroke is a medical emergency. Signs are hot skin that is not sweating, fast breathing, a fast heartbeat, confusion, clumsiness, a seizure, or passing out.

## What you need

- Cool water and a cloth or sheet
- Water or a sports drink to drink
- Cold packs or ice, if you have them

## Steps

1. Move the person to a cool, shaded place.
2. Remove clothes they don't need, such as a jacket, shoes and socks.
3. Spray or sponge their skin with cool water, and fan them.
4. Put cold packs wrapped in cloth under the armpits or on the neck.
5. Give them plenty of water to drink, in frequent sips. A sports drink or rehydration drink also helps.
6. Stay with them. They should start to feel better within 30 minutes.
7. Call the emergency number if they are not better after 30 minutes, or show signs of heatstroke.
8. For heatstroke, cool them fast: wrap them in a cool, wet sheet, or soak their clothes with cool water.
9. Put cold wet cloths or ice on the head, neck, armpits and groin.
10. If they pass out, put them on their side in the recovery position and tell the ambulance service.
11. If they show no signs of life, start [CPR](article:cpr).

## Warnings

- In heatstroke, body temperature can rise to about 41 °C (106 °F) or higher within 10 to 15 minutes. Act fast.
- Do not drive yourself to the hospital if you have signs of heatstroke. Call an ambulance.
- Do not leave the person alone until they are better or help arrives.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting too long. Not better after 30 minutes of rest, cooling and drinks means it is an emergency.
- Missing the signs on brown or black skin, where pale or red skin is harder to see.

## Get help

Heat exhaustion usually does not need emergency help if the person cools down within 30 minutes. Get medical advice if you are struggling to treat it. Call the emergency number for any sign of heatstroke.
