## When to use

Use this when the tap water stops, or the authorities say it is not safe to drink.

## What you need

- Stored drinking water
- Clean containers with lids, and a clean scoop
- A way to [boil water](article:boiling-water) or [disinfect it with bleach](article:bleach-water)

## Steps

1. Follow what the local authorities say, such as boil-water notices.
2. Drink your stored water first. See [Emergency water storage](article:emergency-water-storage).
3. Do not ration drinking water unless the authorities tell you to. Drink what you need, rest and stay cool.
4. Use melted ice cubes made from safe water, and clear water from the toilet tank, not the bowl.
5. Use water drained from the hot water tank for washing. Switch off its power first.
6. Use pool or hot tub water only for washing and cleaning, never for drinking.
7. Boil or disinfect any water you are not sure about before you use it.
8. Take stored water out with a clean scoop. Do not dip your hands in it.
9. If you can't flush, make an [emergency toilet](article:emergency-toilet).
10. Keep your hands clean. See [Washing hands without running water](article:handwashing).

## Warnings

- **Never drink water from radiators or heating boilers.**
- Boiling and bleach can't make water safe if it contains fuel or chemicals. Find another source.
- Do not use unsafe water to brush teeth, wash dishes or food, make ice, or mix baby formula.
- Drink water, not soda or caffeinated drinks.

## Common mistakes

- Rationing water so much that people get dehydrated. See [Dehydration](article:dehydration).
- Using toilet bowl water or water with cleaner in it.

## Get help

Ask the local authorities or health department where to get safe water, and when the tap water is safe again.
