## When to use

Use this when there is a fire or smoke in your home. You may have less than 2 minutes to get out after the smoke alarm sounds.

## What you need

- Working smoke alarms
- Two ways out of every room
- A meeting place outside, in front of the home

## Steps

1. Shout "Fire!" to warn everyone, and help each other get out.
2. Get out right away.
3. Crawl low under the smoke. Smoke and poisonous gases gather near the ceiling first.
4. Before opening a door, feel the door and its handle. If hot, or smoke comes around it, use another way out.
5. Open doors slowly, ready to shut them fast if there is heavy smoke or fire.
6. In an apartment building, close your door behind you, pull the fire alarm, and use the stairs, not the elevator.
7. Go to your meeting place outside and check that everyone is out.
8. Call the fire department. Give the exact address.
9. Tell the firefighters if anyone, or any pet, is still inside, and where.
10. Stay outside at the meeting place.

## Warnings

- If you can't get out: close the door, cover vents and gaps with cloth or tape, and call the emergency number. Say where you are.
- If trapped, signal from the window with a light-colored cloth or a flashlight.
- If your clothes catch fire: stop, drop to the ground, cover your face and roll until the flames are out. Then see [Burns and scalds](article:burns).
- Only fight a fire while it is still small. If it grows fast, get out. Smoke is very toxic.

## Common mistakes

- Children hiding from firefighters. Teach them not to.
- Opening a hot door.
- Going back inside.

## Get help

Prepare before a fire: put smoke alarms on every level of the home and test them. Replace the batteries twice a year, unless they are 10-year batteries. Practice your escape plan twice a year, and sleep with bedroom doors closed.
