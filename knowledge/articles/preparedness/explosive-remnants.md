## When to use

Use this if you see a suspected bomb, shell, mine, or other suspicious object in an area affected by fighting. Weapons that did not explode can still kill or injure people.

## What you need

- A way to contact local police or emergency services from safety

## Steps

1. Stop when you notice a suspicious object.
2. Do not approach or touch it.
3. Look at your surroundings and slowly move back.
4. Warn other people to stay away.
5. Report the object to local police or emergency services.
6. Follow their instructions.

## Warnings

- Do not touch, move, or disturb suspected explosive objects.
- Explosive weapons can remain dangerous after fighting has ended.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming an unexploded weapon is safe because it did not go off.
- Approaching a suspicious object to examine it.

## Get help

Contact local police or emergency services from safety. If someone is bleeding, see [severe bleeding](article:severe-bleeding) only when you can help without entering danger.
