## When to use

Use this to plan for your pets before and during an emergency. If you must leave home, your pets should leave with you.

## What you need

- Several days' food and water for each pet, in waterproof containers, and bowls
- Extra medicine, a leash or harness, and a carrier for each pet
- Litter and litter box, plastic bags and paper towels
- Vaccination records and a photo of you with your pet

## Steps

1. Make sure each pet wears a collar with an ID tag, and is microchipped with your current contact details.
2. Find out where you can take pets. Many public shelters and hotels do not allow them.
3. Ask a neighbor, friend or relative to care for or evacuate your pets if you can't.
4. Pack a pet emergency kit, and check the food and medicine regularly.
5. Train your pet to go into its carrier, and learn where it hides.
6. Bring pets indoors at the first sign of a storm or disaster.
7. If you evacuate, take your pets with you.
8. If you shelter at home, keep pets in an inside room with few windows and no chemicals.
9. After the emergency, keep pets on a leash or in a carrier when outside.
10. Check your home for sharp objects, spilled chemicals and loose wires before letting pets roam.

## Warnings

- **Never leave pets behind or chained outdoors.** They may be lost, injured or killed.
- Do not let pets drink floodwater or other dirty water.
- Wash your hands after touching your pet, its food or its waste.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming emergency shelters will take pets.
- Letting a frightened pet loose in a damaged area.

## Get help

If your pet goes missing, tell staff at animal shelters and report it to the microchip company. Ask a veterinarian if your pet seems stressed or ill.
