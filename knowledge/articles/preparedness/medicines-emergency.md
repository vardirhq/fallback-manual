## When to use

Use this when an emergency may interrupt medicine supplies, refrigeration, or power for medical equipment. Plan with your healthcare provider before an emergency if possible.

## What you need

- Your medicines and medical supplies
- A written list of medicines, doses, and allergies
- Contact details for your healthcare provider and pharmacy

## Steps

1. Ask your doctor or pharmacist about an emergency medicine supply.
2. List your medicines, doses, medical supply needs, and allergies.
3. Keep the list with your emergency supplies.
4. Identify a backup power source for equipment that needs electricity.
5. Ask your healthcare provider how to store medicines that need refrigeration.
6. Take your medicines and medical aids if you must evacuate.
7. Contact your healthcare provider or pharmacy if your supply is running low.

## Warnings

- Power failures can be life-threatening for people who depend on electric medical equipment.
- Do not assume a medicine remains effective after incorrect storage; urgently ask a pharmacist or doctor about continuing treatment.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving without necessary medicines or medical aids.
- Waiting until supplies run out before contacting a healthcare provider.

## Get help

Contact your doctor or pharmacist for advice about specific medicines and equipment. If a power failure threatens life or health, contact emergency services.
