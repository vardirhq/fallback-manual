## When to use

Use this when authorities tell you to evacuate, or when an emergency makes it unsafe to stay home. The safe place to go depends on the emergency.

## What you need

- A phone, identification and important contact numbers
- Medicines and necessary medical aids
- Warm clothing, food and water
- Payment cards and some cash
- Supplies for pets, if needed

## Steps

1. Leave immediately if officials tell you to evacuate.
2. Follow official instructions about where to go and which route to take.
3. Take your emergency kit, medicines and medical aids if you can do so safely without delaying.
4. Take your pets and their supplies if you can do so safely.
5. Follow designated evacuation routes; shortcuts may be blocked.
6. Keep listening to official updates about the emergency and when it is safe to return.

## Warnings

- Do not delay leaving to gather belongings when there is immediate danger.
- Do not return until authorities say it is safe.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting until an emergency to decide where to go and how to get there.
- Forgetting medicines, medical aids or pets when planning to leave.
- Taking a shortcut that may be blocked.

## Get help

If you need help leaving, contact local emergency services or follow local officials' instructions. Plan ahead with family, friends or local authorities if you need assistance with transport.
