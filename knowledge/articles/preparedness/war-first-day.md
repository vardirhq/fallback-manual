## When to use

Use this when war or an attack threatens your area. The safest place depends on the danger and official instructions.

## What you need

- A radio or phone for official updates, if available
- Available medicines, water, and warm clothes

## Steps

1. Seek nearby cover immediately if an attack warning sounds.
2. Listen for local instructions on a radio or phone.
3. Follow instructions to shelter or evacuate.
4. Gather medicines and necessary medical aids if you must leave.
5. Take identification, warm clothes, food, and drink if you must leave.
6. Check the available drinking water and food if you can remain safely.
7. Arrange help with family, friends, or neighbors.

## Warnings

- Do not travel a long distance for shelter during an immediate attack.
- The safest response changes with the threat. Follow local instructions.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting near windows during an attack.
- Assuming that staying home is always safe.

## Get help

For immediate cover, see [air raid](article:air-raid). For leaving, see [evacuation](article:evacuation). Contact local emergency services when help is urgently needed.
