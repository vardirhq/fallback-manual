## When to use

Use this to prepare before an emergency interrupts water, electricity or access to supplies. Adapt your supplies to the people and animals in your household.

## What you need

- Stored drinking water and food that keeps at room temperature
- A flashlight, radio and spare batteries or another power source
- Regular medicines, first aid and hygiene supplies
- Warm clothing and blankets

## Steps

1. Gather drinking water and shelf-stable food for everyone in your household.
2. Include food and water for pets.
3. Add a manual can opener if your food needs one.
4. Pack a flashlight, radio, spare batteries and a charged phone power bank.
5. Include medicines, first aid items and the hygiene products your household needs.
6. Add a paper list of important phone numbers, payment cards and some cash.
7. Keep a smaller portable kit where you can take it if you need to leave.
8. Review your household's needs and update your supplies regularly.

## Warnings

- Never use generators, grills or camping stoves inside a home or garage. See [Carbon monoxide poisoning](article:carbon-monoxide).
- Plan a safe place to stay if your home has no alternative heat source.

## Common mistakes

- Forgetting supplies for pets or household members with specific needs.
- Having canned food but no manual can opener.
- Relying only on a phone for emergency information and contact numbers.

## Get help

Check local authorities' information for emergency updates. Family, friends and neighbors can help each other prepare.
