## When to use

Use this before an emergency, so everyone in your household knows how to reach each other and where to meet if you are separated.

## What you need

- Paper and pen, or a printed form
- Phone numbers and addresses for everyone in your household

## Steps

1. Talk with your household about how you will get emergency alerts.
2. Agree where to shelter at home, and how you would leave.
3. Choose a meeting place that is familiar and easy to find.
4. Write down phone numbers and email addresses for every household member and your key contacts.
5. Add each school, child care and workplace: address, phone number and pick-up plan.
6. Write down medical information: doctors, allergies, prescriptions, assistive devices and your veterinarian.
7. Agree who will help whom, such as young children, older people and anyone with a disability.
8. Plan for pets and service animals.
9. Keep the plan where everyone can find it.
10. Practice the plan with your household.

## Warnings

- Plan ahead for anyone who depends on medical devices, medicines or help from others.
- Check whether your [emergency supplies](article:emergency-supplies) need updating when you update the plan.

## Common mistakes

- Making a plan but never practicing it.
- Forgetting pets in the plan. See [Pets in an emergency](article:pets).

## Get help

Ask neighbors, friends and relatives whether they can help with communication, child care or pets in an emergency, and agree how.
