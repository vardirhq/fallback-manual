## When to use

Use this when you can't use the toilet, for example when the water supply fails.

## What you need

- Strong plastic bags, such as carrier bags or trash bags
- A bucket, or the toilet bowl
- Toilet paper
- Wet wipes, hand sanitizer and disposable gloves

## Steps

1. Put a double plastic bag over the toilet seat, or in a bucket.
2. Use it like a normal toilet.
3. Tie the bags closed with a double knot.
4. Put them in your regular household trash.
5. Clean your hands well after using the toilet.
6. Clean your hands again before you prepare food.

## Warnings

- Poor hygiene spreads illness and bacteria. Be strict about clean hands after the toilet and before food.
- Water drained from a hot water tank is fine for washing, but do not drink it. Unplug the tank before you drain it.
- After a nuclear or chemical accident, rainwater and river water may be heavily contaminated. Ask the authorities before using it for washing.

## Common mistakes

- Not keeping enough bags, toilet paper and hand sanitizer at home.
- Leaving trash and food scraps lying around. They attract bacteria and pests.

## Get help

Follow advice from the authorities about the water supply, and how long it will be out.
