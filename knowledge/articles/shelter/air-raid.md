## When to use

Use this if an air raid warning sounds or officials tell you to take cover during an attack. Choose a nearby protected place before an emergency if you can.

## What you need

- A nearby shelter or protected place
- A radio or phone for official updates, if available

## Steps

1. Move quickly and carefully to a nearby designated shelter or protected space.
2. If none is immediately nearby, take cover in the building you are in or the nearest building.
3. Use a basement or an interior room if there is no accessible shelter.
4. Stay away from windows.
5. Listen for official instructions on a radio or phone.
6. Follow local instructions about when to leave or evacuate.

## Warnings

- Do not travel a long distance to reach a shelter during an immediate threat.
- Explosions can shatter windows and cause injury from flying glass.
- Different threats require different responses. Follow local instructions if they differ from this guide.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting near windows to watch an attack.
- Ignoring instructions to evacuate when authorities say the area is unsafe.

## Get help

Follow local authorities for shelter locations and emergency instructions. If you must leave, see [evacuation](article:evacuation). For supplies, see [emergency supplies](article:emergency-supplies).
