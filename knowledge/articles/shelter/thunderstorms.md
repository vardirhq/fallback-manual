## When to use

Use this when a thunderstorm is coming or you hear thunder. If you can hear thunder, lightning can reach you. There is no safe place outdoors in a thunderstorm.

## What you need

- A building with electricity and plumbing, or a car with a hard metal roof

## Steps

1. When you hear thunder, go indoors right away.
2. Shelter in a building with electricity and plumbing, or a hard-top car with the windows closed.
3. Stay away from windows and doors, and off porches and balconies.
4. Do not use anything plugged into an outlet. Mobile and cordless phones are safe.
5. Avoid water: do not shower, bathe or wash dishes.
6. Do not lie on concrete floors or lean against concrete walls.
7. Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder before going outside again.
8. If caught outside, get off hills and ridges, and away from water, tall trees, fences and power lines.
9. In a group outdoors, spread out.
10. In strong wind or hail, go into a sturdy building and stay away from windows.
11. After the storm, watch for fallen power lines and trees, and report them.

## Warnings

- Tents, sheds, gazebos, picnic shelters and open-sided buildings do **not** protect you from lightning.
- Never shelter under a lone tree or a rock overhang, and never lie flat on the ground.
- Do not drive through flooded roads. See [Flooding](article:flooding).
- A person struck by lightning carries no electric charge and is safe to touch.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting too long to go indoors.
- Going back outside too soon after the storm.

## Get help

If someone is struck by lightning, call the emergency number right away. Check their breathing, and if they are not breathing normally, start [CPR](article:cpr). Then check for burns and other injuries.
