## When to use

Use this when you need to shelter during repeated attacks or for a long period. Follow the instructions for your location.

## What you need

- Drinking water and food
- Medicines and medical aids
- Warm clothes or blankets
- Radio or phone, if available

## Steps

1. Move to a nearby protected place when warned.
2. Stay away from windows.
3. Keep medicines and medical aids with you.
4. Keep drinking water and food with you if available.
5. Ask shelter staff about toilet and hygiene arrangements.
6. Wash your hands with soap and water when available.
7. Keep children close and give them quiet activities.
8. Listen for official updates.
9. Follow local instructions about when to leave.

## Warnings

- Do not delay taking cover to collect supplies during an immediate attack.
- Do not assume a shelter is appropriate for every kind of emergency.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving shelter without checking local instructions.
- Forgetting needed medicines and medical aids.

## Get help

Ask shelter staff or local authorities for help with supplies or medical needs. See [air raid](article:air-raid) and [emergency toilet](article:emergency-toilet).
